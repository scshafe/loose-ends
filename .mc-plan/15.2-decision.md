Phase 15.2 keeps the service core build-only and connection-safe in this container: `serve` constructs a pgx pool but does not ping the database on startup, and this turn did not run `serve` or `migrate`. Migrations define the §6 tables and indexes but intentionally rely on the already-provisioned `pgcrypto` and `citext` extensions rather than creating extensions from the application migration. The migration runner uses golang-migrate's pgx/v5 driver and maps the app's `postgres://` DSN to the driver's `pgx5://` scheme.
